# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to make prompts better through honest, specific critique.
That only works if criticism stays aimed at the artifact and never at the
person. Everyone participating — issues, pull requests, discussions — is
expected to keep that line.

**Encouraged:** direct technical disagreement, "this section earns nothing,
cut it", asking someone to justify an addition, changing your mind in public.

**Not tolerated:** personal attacks, harassment of any kind, demeaning or
discriminatory comments, publishing others' private information, and sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers via a private security advisory or the
contact address in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may temporarily or permanently ban accounts for repeated or severe
violations.
